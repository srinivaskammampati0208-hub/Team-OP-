How HealAI works

HealAI combines guided image capture, progress tracking, and AI-assisted pattern recognition to support wound monitoring.

📱

1. Guided Image Capture

Users are guided through consistent image capture to reduce changes caused by lighting, distance, and camera angle.

🧠

2. AI-Assisted Analysis

The platform reviews visible patterns and tracks changes that may be useful for monitoring and screening support.

📊

3. Healing Timeline

Images, notes, symptoms, and check-ins are organized into a clear timeline for review.

What the photo scanner checks

Each upload is screened on-device from the image pixels. Random photos are rejected; close-up wound photos are verified or flagged for review.

1

Quality

Blur, darkness, glare, and resolution are scored first.

2

Skin context

The scanner looks for skin and ignores sky, grass, or screens.

3

Wound region

Red, yellow, and dark tissue must form a concentrated cluster.

4

Review notes

Usable photos get monitoring notes to share with a clinician.

A simple monitoring loop

Built for everyday use, with enough structure for care teams.

1

Capture

2

Compare

3

Understand

Receive monitoring-oriented insights.

4

Discuss
