    Capture checklist

    Better photos make verification more reliable.

    • Use daylight or a lamp from the side, not a harsh flash.
    • Hold 15–25 cm away and keep the camera parallel to the skin.
    • Fill the frame with the wound and a little surrounding skin.
    • Remove extra clothing or bedding from the background.
    • If a dressing is covering the wound, only uncover it if a clinician has said it is safe.
