Helpful resources

Practical information to support safer wound monitoring and better conversations with healthcare professionals.

💡

Image Capture Tips

Use consistent lighting, keep the camera steady, and capture images from a similar distance during each check-in.

📝

What to Track

Record changes in size, color, discomfort, swelling, drainage, and other symptoms.

🚨

When to Seek Care

Contact a qualified healthcare professional if symptoms worsen or if you have concerns about your wound.

Make each check-in more useful

Small habits help create a clearer picture over time.

☀️

Keep conditions similar

Try to take photos at the same time of day, with the same background and camera distance, so changes are easier to compare.

📒

Add short notes

A sentence about pain, itchiness, drainage, or dressing changes can help your care team understand what the image alone cannot show.

💬

Prepare for appointments

Bring your timeline, recent photos, and questions so conversations with a clinician are more focused and informed.

Warning signs to share promptly

Contact a qualified clinician if any of these appear or worsen.

Spreading redness, increasing warmth, or a red streak moving away from the wound.

New or worsening pain, swelling, fever, chills, or feeling unwell.

Thick, green, or foul-smelling drainage, or tissue that turns dark or black.

The wound opens further, bleeds heavily, or a dressing soaks through repeatedly.

If you believe you are experiencing a medical emergency, contact local emergency services immediately. HealAI is not an emergency-care service.
